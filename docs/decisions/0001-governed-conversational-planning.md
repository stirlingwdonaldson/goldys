# Governed conversational planning

Accepted with the owner on 12 November 2026. Evolve Ask Goldy's toward ontology-assisted planning
over approved business concepts, with application code validating and authorizing structured plans
and executing authoritative calculations. Weekly reporting and exports retain priority; equivalent
requests across chat, dashboards and exports must share business definitions rather than acquire
separate calculation paths. NVIDIA Auto Ontology is an optional discovery/authoring/retrieval
component, to adopt only after a bounded comparison demonstrates benefits over extending Goldy's.

The assistant may compose approved concepts without a dedicated tool for each question. New
definitions and relationships require review. Unsupported analysis explains the limitation and
offers labelled alternatives or proposed extensions; it never silently substitutes dates or
measures. Permissions apply to metadata, execution, follow-ups and saved analyses. Explicit denied
requests are not silently filtered. Arithmetic belongs in governed calculations, not generated prose.

The first implementation exposes the existing metric catalogue and bounded compositions of its
queries. It retains the existing enum tool boundary. The relational query registry is not yet an
authoritative metric ontology: connecting its field aggregations directly would introduce competing
definitions. Subsequent slices must align those semantics, add reviewed relationships and filters,
and preserve structured follow-up scope with reauthorization before extending that boundary.

Neither prototype established production readiness. No NVIDIA integration, live-model evaluation,
source access or production operation is authorized by this architecture record. Implementation was
authorized separately; experiments and publishing remain subject to their own authorization.
