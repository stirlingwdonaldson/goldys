# Product scope decisions

Owner answers recorded during the scope review, 4 November 2026.

These record target scope and workflow choices, not production approvals, manager grants or acceptance certification.

- **DOC-001:** Reconcile the original app PRD and independent PRD/SRS; retain distinct requirements and seek decisions on conflicts; proposals remain explicit.
- **DOC-002:** Active product scope covers the current pub only; second-pub planning and live multi-venue operation are out of active scope.
- **DOC-003:** Target scope includes weekly reporting, reusable custom reports, and CSV/Excel exports from business views.
- **DOC-004:** Target forecasting scope includes overall revenue and individual-product sales forecasts; delivery and acceptance are not yet established.
- **DOC-005:** Labour reporting remains in product scope. Weekly-report inclusion is gated on reliable source data and validated meaning.
- **DOC-006:** Target stock scope includes usage and unexplained variance analysis, gated on supported movements and unit evidence.
- **DOC-007:** Target purchasing scope includes shortage warnings and suggested order quantities for human review. Orders remain in external systems.
- **DOC-008:** Target costing scope includes theoretical recipe costs and actual inventory-based COGS/gross margin. Actual COGS requires validated stock movements and valuation rules.
- **DOC-009:** Labour scope is roster, hours and cost analysis only. Staffing-level recommendations are excluded from active scope; rostering remains external.
- **DOC-010:** Target labour cost reporting includes wages and separately labelled full employment cost, gated on validated cost components.
- **DOC-011:** Reservation scope is bookings and recorded reservation outcomes only. Independent actual-attendance tracking including walk-ins is excluded from active scope; booked party sizes must not be represented as verified attendance.
- **DOC-012:** Customer-review scope includes ratings, full review text, recurring themes and sentiment, traceable to original reviews.
- **DOC-013:** Target financial reporting includes operating profit when complete supporting expense data and validated meanings are available.
- **DOC-014:** Local workflows include assigning operational follow-ups to people and tracking completion, in addition to notes and data-quality review. This does not authorize source-system write-back.
- **DOC-015:** Target scope includes physical cash and processor-to-bank settlement reconciliation, gated on independent supporting evidence.
- **DOC-016:** Target application access is owners and managers only; staff accounts/access are excluded from active scope. Specific manager permission grants remain to be agreed.
- **DOC-017:** Target automation includes scheduled report delivery and condition-based alerts via notifications or exports. Connected source systems remain read-only.
- **DOC-018:** Target supplier oversight includes order/delivery/invoice/credit matching and supplier payment-status reporting when supported. No order placement or payment execution.
- **DOC-019:** Safety, licensing, staff-competency and incident-control functionality is retained as a future candidate outside active product scope.
- **DOC-020:** Service-timing and order-to-fulfilment stage/delay analysis is excluded from active product scope. Receipt-close timestamps must not be treated as fulfilment evidence.
- **DOC-021:** Live report views remain available. Issued or sent weekly/custom reports retain exact figures and qualifications as dated versions; corrections create new versions.
- **DOC-022:** All manager permission mappings remain undecided. Owner/manager target access does not authorize inferred manager grants.
- **DOC-023:** Trustworthy weekly reporting and exports lead implementation delivery after documentation and skill cleanup. Other accepted capabilities remain in target scope.
- **DOC-024:** Customer balances, outstanding tabs, deposits, vouchers and credit/liability reporting remain undecided; no delivery commitment or exclusion is inferred.
- **DOC-025:** Preserve current visual style and desktop-first use; improve owner/manager workflows. Full phone usability and a new visual direction are not adopted.
- **DOC-026:** Matt Pocock selected skills become the primary planning workflow. Retire Superpowers as the default; retain Ponytail and all six Goldys implementation recipes.
- **DOC-027:** Payroll obligations, payroll payments and payroll settlement reporting stay in the payroll system; Goldys scope remains roster, hours and cost analysis.
- **DOC-028:** Prime cost is a target measure once actual COGS and labour cost are both validated on comparable scope and periods. This does not authorize weekly-report inclusion before labour readiness.
