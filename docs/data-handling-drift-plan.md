### Attendance and time — OD-03 / OD-09

- Staff rarely update booked party size to match who actually arrived. Treat this as intended size and any attendance-derived sum as a labelled booking-size proxy, not verified actual covers. Actual-cover-dependent KPIs cannot become verified by changing labels.
- A no-show is a business declaration that the booked people did not arrive after their scheduled booking time. A cancellation is a customer cancellation before the scheduled time. Preserve declared status and its time; do not infer no-show merely from absent arrival evidence. Walk-ins are not expected bookings. Exact assessment grace period and handling of post-scheduled cancellations/status conflicts remain unspecified.
- Timezone: **Australia/Melbourne**. Reporting week: **Monday–Sunday**, using the adopted trading-date attribution for sales.
- Apply a simplified **02:00 local trading-day cutoff on every weekday**, explicitly flagging it as the owner's simplification based on the licence's latest time. Thus 01:00 Saturday belongs to Friday sales. Proposed exact boundary interpretation: [02:00, next 02:00); a sale exactly at 02:00 belongs to the new trading date. Exact-boundary convention still needs confirmation. Do not alter the source close instant, invoice/accounting dates or payroll dates. Date-only daily aggregates cannot be shifted accurately without timestamp/source-period evidence; do not shift an already attributed source trading date twice.

### Money and presentation — OD-07 / OD-08 / OD-09

- Business performance generally presents **sales excluding GST**, retaining inclusive amounts where useful. Derive exclusive amounts only from evidenced exclusive/tax components, never a blanket GST assumption.
- Rename purchasing representations from COGS to **Invoice purchases**. **Remove Food cost % for now**, retaining a flagged follow-up for properly supported cost/valuation semantics.
- Labour cost currently means **wages only**, prominently qualified as excluding other employment costs pending clarification. This adopts the intended display scope, not proof that every existing source aggregate is wages-only; verify the source basis before presenting it as such. No hours-times-rate calculation or loaded-cost model is authorised by this decision.
- Display money to cents and **whole percentages**, explicitly confirmed by the user. Display rounding does not establish calculation precision or reconciliation tolerance; those remain open. Test eligibility against unrounded values before rounding the display.
