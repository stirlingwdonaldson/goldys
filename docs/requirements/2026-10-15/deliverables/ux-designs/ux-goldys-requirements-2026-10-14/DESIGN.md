# Goldy's — Design Spine

## Brand & Style

This companion preserves the normal BMAD spine pair while limiting visual decisions to what the behavioural specification requires. No palette, logo, font, UI library, theme, decorative treatment or token scale has been selected. Empty token objects mean **not selected**, not implementation defaults. This is a complete minimal contract for the requested documentation scope, not a complete visual identity or a build-ready design system.

[EXPERIENCE.md](EXPERIENCE.md) governs the three responsibility contexts, decision hierarchy, permissions, states, journeys and responsive behaviour. This file governs presentation constraints. Visual design must make question, result, qualification, permitted response and feedback easy to distinguish. It must not introduce business priority, authority or certainty through decoration.

## Colors

No colour values are specified. Status and comparison meaning must appear in text and structure as well as any later colour treatment. Do not use future positive/negative colours to imply a favourable direction or performance target that has not been agreed. Contrast pairings and formal conformance scope must be checked when visual tokens are selected; no contrast or accessibility certification is claimed here.

## Typography

No font family or type scale is specified. Unit, period, evidence status and warning text must remain readable alongside the value at every supported width and user text size. Do not reduce these to fine print or clip them to preserve a large KPI numeral. Numbers and labels in exported reports must retain their meaning after formatting is removed.

## Layout & Spacing

Use the relative compositions in EXPERIENCE.md GUX-06–08 and the reading order in GUX-12. On larger website surfaces, allow evidence beside the decision when space permits. On phones, stack decision, qualification, evidence, action and feedback without losing meaning. Exact grids, dimensions and breakpoints remain unselected.

Give Weekly review and Operational overview equal prominence at initial Upper management entry; neither has a preselected default or visual recommendation. FOH/BOH candidate modules have no agreed business rank. Staff and manager compositions differ according to duties and grants, rather than through decorative role badges. Supplementary technical details belong behind labelled disclosure; decision-changing caveats stay visible.

## Components

The names below match EXPERIENCE.md Component Patterns. These are presentation constraints and anatomy; interaction/state contracts remain in that file. No mockups were requested; all surfaces are specification-only.

| Component | Presentation anatomy and constraints |
|---|---|
| Context and scope header | Readable context, department/area, interval, comparator, as-of and revision/status. Keep labels visible during narrow reflow; no ambiguous venue/date-only heading |
| Navigation and home choice | Text labels for the three contexts and applicable destinations. Equal treatment for the two Upper management alternatives; visible selected context without suggesting a grant |
| Decision summary | Question, result, qualification and response grouped together. Avoid decorative KPI tiles detached from a decision |
| Measure and comparison | Value next to unit/status, clearly named comparator, coverage and basis. No unlabeled arrow, isolated percentage or colour-only change |
| Exception notice | Label, affected result/question, consequence and response remain legible and adjacent. No tiny warning icon standing in for the explanation |
| Evidence explanation | Clearly separated explanation, definition/components and original evidence. Distinguish original fact, local proposal and revisions visually and in text |
| Business question controls | Persistent labels, visible selection/filter summary and available comparison basis. Selected filters cannot be hidden by the narrow layout |
| Evidence list | Meaningful row/column labels, readable units/statuses and visible population/count. Keep row identity understandable in stacked or scrollable presentation |
| Forecast and scenario comparison | Explicit labels for historical actual, issued forecast and local scenario; assumptions and uncertainty beside outputs. No styling that makes projected values look observed |
| Local record and follow-up | Explicit local-record label, ownership/verification state and source link; Save/Cancel and failed-save message visible. Completion claim and verified closure remain distinct |
| Export review | Scope/revision/format recap, caveats, omissions, progress and file result. Export status must not look like recipient delivery or report approval |
| Disclosure and dialog | Clear heading, labelled trigger/close action and distinguishable focus. Supplementary detail remains recognisably subordinate; avoid stacked overlays |
| Save and progress feedback | Legible editing/saving/saved/failed state near the affected record; do not communicate successful persistence with an icon alone |

## Do's and Don'ts

- Preserve responsibility context and the user's operational question when introducing any later visual design.
- Show known zero, unknown, not captured, disputed, not applicable and unavailable as different text states.
- Preserve critical labels and qualifications in phone, zoomed, printed and exported presentations.
- Keep source/diagnostic administration secondary unless it is the active authorised responsibility.
- Do not select branding or populate token values by copying the existing application. No implementation was inspected.
- Do not treat this file's completion as selection of visual tokens or approval of candidate business priorities. EXPERIENCE.md contains no unresolved token references because no visual tokens have been selected.
