# Goldy's PRD — Coaching and Validation Addendum

This companion to [prd.md](prd.md) preserves coaching prompts, source limitations and the completion audit. All normative business requirements, minimum data contracts, acceptance criteria, non-goals and open decisions are in the PRD. This addendum introduces no additional product scope, priority, permission or implementation prescription.

## Coaching context and new evidence

The user requested Create intent, Coaching mode and journey-led organisation, with comprehensive candidate derivation and all Product Brief stakeholder decisions left open. The existing PRD starter was resumed. The user was invited to add stakeholder context or a real session; they reported that owner and general manager primarily want automation of weekly cross-platform reporting into a spreadsheet, revenue and product-sales forecasting, quick actionable insights and CSV/formatted Excel exports.

That response is recorded as S06 and informs BG-01, UJ-01/UJ-02 and the report/forecast/export requirements. It narrows OD-01 without pretending that the weekly report's contents, calculation bases, deadlines, access, success targets or priorities across other decisions have been validated. No stakeholder session was narrated and no real names were supplied. Journey names remain illustrative; no current operating process is claimed from them.

The charter and latest user instruction permit completing a candidate PRD despite unresolved stakeholder decisions. Mechanical drafting, evidence reconciliation and review were sequential. No new web research, multi-agent work, application inspection, implementation planning or technical design was performed during this completion run. No source document was rewritten to match the PRD's proposals.

## Prompts for later stakeholder validation

These prompts explain what evidence is needed, rather than supplying answers. They are not a request to begin another workflow now.

### Weekly report — OD-01, OD-03, OD-09, OD-13

Ask the report preparer to walk through the latest real weekly spreadsheet: what decision each number supports, where it comes from, how long assembly/checking takes, which totals differ, which basis/date rules are used and what happens when a platform is late. Ask the owner and GM which sections they actually act on, which decisions remain unsupported and what they need to see first. Distinguish recurring report preparation from distribution or approval.

Review one ordinary week and one correction/partial-source week. Agree period boundaries, data and measure scope, comparison logic, cadence, readiness deadline, quality states, readers/sign-off, export presentation and reissue history. An existing business report may be examined as stakeholder evidence without examining the application repository or adopting its implementation.

### Quick questions and exports — OD-02, OD-10, OD-12, OD-13

Have a recipient describe a real question, the authorised information needed, expected working conditions and what action would follow. Ask them to inspect a CSV and formatted Excel example for usable units, dates, numeric types, readable labels, missing states and supporting evidence. Validate acceptable task time and recipient-tool compatibility instead of inventing a universal “fast” target.

Decide which roles may see individual employee/pay/customer data versus aggregates. Confirm whether a report is only prepared/exported or also distributed, and establish recipients, method and authority separately. A local export remains accessible after download; the platform cannot promise retroactive recall.

### Forecasts — OD-04, OD-05, OD-09, OD-13

Ask which revenue definition, products, variants, sale units, horizon and decision deadline matter. Ask what the user would do differently at different forecast values, what uncertainty would prevent action, and how outcomes will be judged. Review issued-before-outcome examples including stockout-constrained history, new products, zero actuals and missing evidence. Establish whether weather or bookings add useful information rather than assuming measured causality.

### Department work — OD-02, OD-04–OD-08, OD-14

Walk through staffing a service, changing a shift after sickness, ordering through the next delivery, accepting/rejecting a receipt, counting stock and resolving an actual discrepancy. Confirm performer, outcome owner, verifier, approver, limits and absence cover separately. Use the reported cocktail, Aperol and parma examples as prompts, not as quantified patterns or prioritised requirements.

Trace each decision to the relevant information and external action, then agree its outcome measure. Distinguish recorded consumption from residual loss, assigned from worked hours, price/recipe cost from final cost, and local follow-up from externally verified action.

### Opening — OD-01, OD-08, OD-11

Ask the owner which opening choices require evidence first and what is known about the new site. For each choice, state current-pub facts, new-site observations, untested transfer assumptions, resource/cost coverage, alternatives and sensitivities. The reported 6–12-month expectation from 14 October 2026 remains an estimate; it is not a committed date or authorisation for live multi-venue operations.

## Research limits preserved

S03 describes independent research, not a Goldy's compliance assessment. Its confidence labels, single-publisher/limited retrieval and applicability limitations remain unresolved. Its record-retention, employment, super, food, liquor, accounting and privacy findings require current authoritative re-verification and venue applicability before adopting operational/legal requirements. This PRD introduces no rate, award, deadline, record-retention duration, control threshold or exemption from those findings. GOL-CON-004 and OD-07/OD-10 govern adoption.

S04's entities/cardinalities and S05's role assignments/KPI conventions remain proposed. The PRD uses them to derive C/CC contracts and explicit acceptance cases, while preserving the charter's baseline coverage and semantic safeguards. Workload, context, local records and report contracts GOL-DR-044–GOL-DR-048 are explicit derived extensions justified by S02/S06; they are not falsely attributed to E44–E48, which do not exist.
