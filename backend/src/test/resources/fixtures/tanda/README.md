# Tanda account-shaped fixture

Captured with GET-only requests on 26 November 2026. Names/contact details, employee IDs,
notes, awards and metadata are omitted; identifiers are remapped consistently and costs rounded.
Retains source break flags, timestamps, nulls, zero costs, pagination and repeated pay-period envelopes.
Real validation: 223 roster records and 6 unique in-window shifts mapped (229/229); three shifts
have unknown worked hours. The capture contains 11 unique shifts across whole pay periods,
of which five precede the validation window. This fixture is not a payroll reconciliation.
