# Dashboard and ranking coverage

Keep resolved reads, stored facts and permissions unchanged. Carry the existing sales-net result notices and provenance through the dashboard instead of dropping them. Label net sales ex GST and distinguish absent/unavailable dates from disputed dates. Historical age alone does not invalidate evidence.

Product rankings share a qualified semantic result across the dashboard, catalogue, saved widgets and existing reporting tools. Coverage is calculated over every resolved row in the requested inclusive range before top-N. A date containing an unavailable amount is unresolved; a date without rows is absent; other represented dates are observed. None proves within-day completeness or the expected trading/product population. Absent product/date combinations never become zero.

Rank known revenue subtotals. Exclude products with no known revenue from numerical ranking and report their count. Explicit zero remains eligible. Always state: “Ranked by known sales; full-period ranking unverified.” Retain name-based identity and existing amount/tax meaning; no cross-source identity or tax assumptions.

Dashboard trend and ranking responses become envelopes so notices survive empty data. Update bootstrap, standalone REST and both frontend implementations together. No persistence or migration changes. No production action is authorised.
