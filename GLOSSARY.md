# Business glossary

- **Purchases:** supplier invoice purchase observations; not actual COGS.
- **Theoretical cost:** an evidenced source/recipe estimate, with its stated cost basis.
- **Actual COGS:** inventory-based cost of goods sold under validated movements and valuation policy.
- **Prime cost:** validated actual COGS plus labour cost on comparable periods and bases.
- **Wages:** the identified wage component; not automatically full employment cost.
- **Full employment cost:** wages plus validated included employment on-cost components.
- **Reservation party size:** the recorded booked/stated group size; not verified actual attendance.
- **Issued report:** a retained dated result with exact figures and qualifications; corrections create a new version.
- **Live dashboard:** saved query configuration rendered against currently authorized data.
- **Follow-up:** an assigned local action/status record; completion does not establish source-system execution.
